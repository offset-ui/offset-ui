## Not covered by the MIT licence

- The Offset UI name.
- The logo and everything in `public/brand` (logo, wordmark, avatar, social preview).
- The visual design of the documentation website: its layout, styling, illustrations and written content.
- The headline fonts in `public/fonts`, which belong to their respective owners (see `public/fonts/README.md`).

## What you can do

- Use, copy, modify and ship the component code in personal and commercial projects. Keep the licence notice at the top of each file.
- Mention Offset UI by name when you describe where a component came from, and link to the project.

## What you cannot do

- Present the Offset UI name, logo or website design as your own.
- Suggest that Offset UI made, approved or endorses your product.
- Change the logo and use it as if it were the original.
- Copy the documentation website's design or content as a site of your own.

## Questions

If you want to use the name or logo in a way that is not listed here, ask first. See the Contact page on the website, or open an issue on GitHub.
