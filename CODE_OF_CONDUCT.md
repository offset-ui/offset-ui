# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarised below.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Offset UI to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and welcoming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting form: <https://github.com/offset-ui/offset-ui/security/advisories/new>. Reports are handled in confidence.
